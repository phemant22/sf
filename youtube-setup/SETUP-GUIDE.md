# Pandey Playz: GTA 6 Channel Setup Kit

Everything here is ready to copy and paste into **YouTube Studio** (studio.youtube.com).
It takes about 20 minutes. Do the steps in order.

---

## Step 1: Upload the banner and profile picture
YouTube Studio → **Customization** → **Branding**

| Item | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Banner image | `banner-2560x1440.png` | Upload, then press **Done**. The text sits inside YouTube's safe area, so it shows on mobile and TV. |
| Profile picture | `profile-800x800.png` | Gets cropped to a circle, and "PP" stays centred. |
| Video watermark | `profile-800x800.png` | Display time: **Entire video**. |

Press **Publish** (top right).

---

Also on this page:

## Step 6: Arrange the home page
Customization → **Layout**
1. **Channel trailer** (for viewers who aren't subscribed): your best GTA 6 video, or a 30–60 second highlight.
2. **Featured video** (for subscribers): your latest GTA 6 video.
3. **Sections, in this order:** Shorts → GTA 6 Story Walkthrough → GTA 6 Tips → Funny Moments → Popular uploads.

## Thumbnails
`thumbnail-template-1.png` and `thumbnail-template-2.png` are 1280×720 layouts.
Open them in **Canva** or **PicsArt** (free), put your face or reaction photo (or a gameplay screenshot) into the dashed box, and change the words.
Rules:
- Use 3 words or fewer.
- Show a big, emotional face.
- Make the text bright yellow or white on a dark background.
